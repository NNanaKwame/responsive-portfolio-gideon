01 / About

Two disciplines, one desk

I'm a full-stack engineer and designer working on user-centric architecture, backend integrations and data analysis. The engineering side is rigorous and cross-platform; the design side is editorial. Most of my best work happens where those two overlap.

That overlap is the reason clients come to me: I can take a project from the first conversation through the visual direction, the build, and the photography that ends up on it — without three separate handovers where the intent gets lost.
